# Code of Conduct

KSquad follows the **CNCF Community Code of Conduct**.

As contributors and maintainers of this project, and in the interest of
fostering an open and welcoming community, we pledge to respect all people who
contribute through reporting issues, posting feature requests, updating
documentation, submitting pull requests or patches, and other activities.

The full, authoritative text is maintained by the Cloud Native Computing
Foundation and applies to all KSquad community spaces (repositories, issue
trackers, chat, and events):

- CNCF Community Code of Conduct:
  https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies both within project spaces and in public spaces
when an individual is representing the project or its community.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the KSquad maintainers (see [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md)).

For incidents that involve a maintainer, that cannot be handled by the project
maintainers, or that the reporter would prefer to escalate, contact the
**CNCF Code of Conduct Committee** at <conduct@cncf.io>. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary
and appropriate to the circumstances. Confidentiality is maintained with regard
to the reporter of an incident.
